## What was missing

They do not explain the normal matched branch's full outgoing total versus its
limited returned count. That distinction matters when a selected declaration
has more stored edges than the response contains. This is a missing required
explanation, rather than a demonstrated false statement.

The same interpretation was applied to all four answers for this task. It does
not require particular keywords: the accepted answers explain the separate
totals, returned subsets and limits through prose or the counted SQL path.
Selections, syntactic-resolution limits and the meaning of ambiguous emptiness
remain supported in the incomplete answers.
